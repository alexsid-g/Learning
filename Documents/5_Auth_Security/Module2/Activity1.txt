LMS Role Design

LSM: CRUD instructors, CRUD students, CRUD lesson, CRUD questionary, CRUD answers. 

1. Admin
    - Full access to mange everything
2. Instructor
    - Create, read, update, delete lessons
    - Create, update, delete, read questionaries
    - Grade assignments
3. Students
    - Read lessons
    - Read questionaries
    - Create, update, delete, read answers to questionaries

Admin - full access.
Instructor - CRUD lessons, CRUD questionaries, RU student answers.
Student - R lessons, CRUD answers.
